Acropolis Attendance Management System (Acro AMS) & Acro CSE Portal

Welcome to Acro AMS, the official secure academic administration, class coordinator tracking, and attendance management portal for the Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) department at Acropolis Institute of Technology and Research (AITR). Designed by lead developer Aayush Sharma, this platform secures attendance logs, maximizes daily logging efficiency, and allows seamless academic tracking.

Core Features & Portals:

  • Acro AMS Faculty Portal Login: Seamlessly choose subject slots, mark student attendance, view class logs, check edit histories, and track syllabus progress.
  • Acropolis Class Coordinator Dashboard: Audit trails, detailed student attendance analysis, batch reporting, parent communication, and final academic performance reports.
  • Acro Student Portal Login: Check subject-wise attendance percentages in real-time, view notifications, track minimum criteria, and contact faculty.
  • Admin Control & System Backup: Secure backup systems, role-based access control, service-worker maintenance, and PWA setup.

Search Keywords and Matching Terms:

This portal is widely searched as Acro AMS, Acropolis Attendance Portal, Acro CSE Attendance, Acro Faculty Portal Login, Acropolis Student Login, AITR Attendance Login, and Acropolis Coordinator Login. It serves 2nd Year and 3rd Year students, faculty coordinators, and department administrators of Acropolis CSE.

Designed and built as a secure Progressive Web App (PWA) using React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript, and Supabase by lead developer Aayush Sharma.
