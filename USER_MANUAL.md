## 1. INTRODUCTION
Acropolis AMS is a high-performance Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to streamline attendance tracking, reporting, and student performance monitoring.

## 2. KEY ROLES & ACCESS
- **Students**: View subject-wise attendance and attendance insights.
- **Faculty**: Mark attendance for assigned subjects and view history.
- **Coordinators**: Access branch-wide reports, monitor faculty activity, and export MST marks.
- **Admin**: Full control over students, faculty, subjects, and system settings.

## 3. CORE FUNCTIONALITIES
### 📱 Marking Attendance
1. Navigate to **Mark Attendance**.
2. Select the Date and Lecture Slot.
3. Toggle students (Green = Present, Red = Absent).
4. Tap **Save Attendance**.

### 📊 Reports & Exports
1. Go to the **Reports** tab (for Coordinators/Admins).
2. Use **MST Marks Summary** to export a side-by-side comparison of marks.
3. Use **Detail Report** to generate attendance percentages for any date range.
4. All reports are exported in professional **Excel (.xlsx)** format.

### ♻️ Data Safety (Recycle Bin)
- Accidentally deleted attendance? Go to the **Menu (Three Bars) > Recycle Bin**.
- You can restore any deleted record with a single click.

### 🕵️ Audit Logs
- Every change is tracked. Admins can view the **Audit Trail** to see exactly who marked or changed any attendance record and at what time.

## 4. INSTALLATION (PWA)
- **Android/Chrome**: Tap the "Install App" button in the menu or the browser prompt.
- **iPhone/Safari**: Tap the **Share** button and select **"Add to Home Screen"**.

## 5. TROUBLESHOOTING
- **App not loading?** Tap "Reload Application" or "Reset Session" from the Error screen.
- **Data not syncing?** Check the status indicator in the top bar. Ensure you have a stable internet connection for the initial sync.
